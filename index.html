AVEAH SYSTEMS

Aveah Systems develops connected software for roofing companies — from inspection and estimating through production, documentation, and closeout.

AVEAHi

Inspection + Estimating Intelligence

AVEAHi helps roofing companies inspect properties, trace roof sections, generate estimates, recommend assemblies and warranties, evaluate weather conditions, and create professional proposals.

MYCOM

Operations + Production Management

MYCOM helps roofing companies manage work after a proposal is approved, including tickets, crew assignments, time tracking, documentation, production visibility, job progress, and closeouts.
